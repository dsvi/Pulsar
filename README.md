Pulsar
======

A simple pulse (heart rate monitor) app for a BLE device.

Does everything you could dream of about such an app:
- Follows android color scheme (Dark, Light)
- Shows pulse and time since start.
- Has "Picture in Picture" mode, to display on top of other apps
- May even show the charge left on the BLE device.
